Assuming the average 1776 transactions per day of the whole dataset for the next 3 months.

Randomnly filled combinations of Country, StockCode and CustomerID are used to predict sales for each future dates, these are validated so all the combinations are valid (ex. no customer with UK as country appears as having Spain as country in the 3 month future prediction).
